Kwiktow NQ is a Townsville family-owned towing and recovery business — built on showing up fast, doing the job right, and treating every customer like a neighbour.

Our story

Built in Townsville, for Townsville.

Kwiktow NQ started with a single tilt tray and a simple idea — that towing in North Queensland should be fast, friendly and fairly priced. Years on, our fleet has grown, but the philosophy hasn't changed.

Whether you're a Townsville local who's locked the keys in the ute outside Coles, a tourist who's blown a tyre on the way to Magnetic Island, or an insurer needing a Cat 1 accident attended at 3am — we're the team that picks up the phone.

Our drivers are trained, our trucks are maintained, and our dispatchers actually know NQ. That's the difference.
