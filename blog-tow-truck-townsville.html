1. After a crash on a Townsville road

If you've been in a collision — Ring Road, Townsville CBD, Bruce Highway through Bohle, or a side street in Aitkenvale — the first call is 000 if anyone is hurt. Once the scene is safe and police are dealt with, you'll need a Townsville tow truck to clear your vehicle.

Accident towing is regulated in Queensland — only authorised operators can attend a crash scene, and you have the right to nominate where your car goes (your panel shop, your home, our compound, anywhere). The truck of choice is a tilt tray so the damaged vehicle isn't dragged.

  • Direct billing to RACQ, AAMI, Suncorp, NRMA, Allianz, QBE, Youi and Budget Direct.
  • Scene clean-up — broken glass, plastic and fluid containment.
  • CCTV-monitored on-site storage if you're not sure where to send the car yet.

3. When you specifically need a tilt tray (not a standard tow)

"Tilt tray" gets thrown around but it's a real distinction. A traditional tow involves wheels-on-the-ground; a tilt tray is a flatbed where the deck pivots down, the vehicle is winched on, and the deck levels back up. It's the right tool when:

  • The vehicle is an AWD or 4WD (towing one axle can damage the centre diff).
  • The vehicle is EV or hybrid (more on that next).
  • The vehicle is low or modified (lowered suspension, big bodykit, splitter — anything that won't clear a standard hookup).
  • The vehicle is damaged from an accident.
  • The vehicle is a prestige or high-value car you simply don't want dragged.

Most of our Townsville fleet is tilt tray for exactly this reason — it covers nearly every passenger-vehicle job safely.

4. EVs and hybrids — why tilt tray is non-negotiable

Townsville's seeing more Teslas, BYDs, MGs, Polestars and Toyota hybrids on the road every month. If yours fails, do not let anyone tow it on its wheels. EVs use the wheels themselves to generate power back into the battery; pulling a non-running EV with two wheels on the ground can damage the motor, battery management system, or both — and that bill is six figures, not four.

Every Kwiktow NQ tilt tray is EV-safe. We winch the vehicle on with neutral handling procedures and secure it without chassis straps that could foul HV components.

6. Prestige, modified and low-clearance vehicles

If your car is worth more than a house deposit, or you've spent a year and your savings on a build — the cheapest tow in town is not the one you want. Prestige, classic, exotic and heavily modified vehicles need:

  • Ramp extensions for low front lips and splitters.
  • Wheel-only restraint (no straps over chassis or control arms).
  • Soft straps that won't mark wheels or paint.
  • Optional covered transport for show cars and customer hand-overs.

11. What to tell the dispatcher when you call

To get a Townsville tow truck dispatched as fast as possible, have these five things ready:

  1. Where you are. A street address is best, but a Maps drop-pin or even "the McDonald's at Aitkenvale, in the carpark facing Ross River Rd" works fine.
  2. Vehicle make, model, year and rough size. "2018 Hilux dual cab" is enough.
  3. What's happened. Won't start? Flat tyre? Crashed? Bogged? Locked out?
  4. Where it's going. Home address, mechanic, dealership, panel shop or "your compound, I'll figure it out tomorrow".
  5. Any access issues. Multi-storey carpark with a height limit? Steep driveway? Locked yard?

That's it. We'll quote on the call, give you an ETA and let you know which truck is heading your way.

12. How to choose a tow truck operator in Townsville

You're searching "tow truck near me" on a phone with 14% battery. Here's the cheat sheet:

  • Is the dispatcher local? Some "Townsville" operators route to a Brisbane call centre that takes 30 seconds longer to find a Townsville suburb on a map. Ask.
  • Are they 24/7 with their own trucks? Plenty of brokers will quote you a price and then sub the job to whoever's free. We run our own fleet.
  • Tilt tray or hook? For nearly every modern car you want tilt tray. If they only offer a hook tow on a 2024 hybrid, hang up.
  • Insurance authorised? If it's an accident, ask — non-authorised operators can't legally attend most scenes.
  • Quote up front. No quote, no booking. We'll give you a real number on the call.
