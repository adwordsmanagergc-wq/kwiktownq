Tilt Tray Towing in Townsville

Our tilt tray trucks are the safest way to move a passenger vehicle without dragging or scuffing the bumper. The bed lowers hydraulically, the car drives or is winched on, and gets transported with all four wheels off the road.

  • Sedans, hatches, utes, 4WDs & light commercial
  • Low-clearance and prestige vehicles handled with care
  • EV-safe — we tow Teslas, BYDs, MGs & hybrids properly
  • Soft straps & wheel-only restraints to protect paint
